How

How owning works.

  1. 01

    You own the house

    You can own the tiny home yourself. The land it sits on is under a lease. This is a legal way to do it.

  2. 02

    We lease you the land

    Foreigners can't own land in Thailand. Anyone selling you "ownership" is selling you a workaround, and every workaround carries its own risk.

  3. 03

    The lease goes on the chanote

    Many people just sign a lease with the landlord, which is okay, but offers no real protection if the land changes hands. We sign a lease with you that is recorded against the chanote, the land title, at the Land Office.

You have a reserved space for your house for the next 30 years. After that, move the house, renew the lease, whatever you want.

Other than a condo, this is the best way to live in Thailand.

What the chanote lease protects

  • If the land is sold, your lease goes with it. The new owner takes the land with you on it.
  • It's a public record at the Land Office, not a piece of paper in a drawer.
  • 30 years. That is the legal maximum for a lease in Thailand.
